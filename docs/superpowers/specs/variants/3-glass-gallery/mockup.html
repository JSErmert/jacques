About

A musician at the edge
of classical and jazz

Jacques is a pianist and composer whose work sits at the boundary between classical structure and jazz's felt freedom. Trained in both traditions, he writes music that is simultaneously rigorous and quietly searching — pieces that ask the listener to stay still, and to notice.
