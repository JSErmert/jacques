About

Rooted in tradition,
open to the night

Jacques grew up with Chopin on vinyl and Coltrane on cassette — two streams that never felt like a contradiction. His compositions move between the formal architecture of classical music and the breathing space of jazz, finding the places where silence says as much as the notes do. Based in San Diego, he performs intimate sets and records quietly for the love of it.
